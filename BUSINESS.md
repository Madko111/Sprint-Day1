# Stacklet Business Frame

## Product
Stacklet is a no-code internal tools builder for operations, support, and revenue teams at fast-growing SaaS companies.

## ICP
- Product operations, sales ops, and customer support leaders at mid-market SaaS companies.
- Teams that need fast, secure internal apps for desk workflows, approval queues, order validation, and cross-team data sync.

## Pain
- Existing internal tools are scattered across spreadsheets, Slack, and legacy admin panels.
- Engineering is overloaded with small operational dashboards.
- Retool is too expensive on a per-user basis and too heavy for non-technical teams.

## Positioning
Stacklet removes engineering bottlenecks with a workspace built for operators.
It connects internal data sources, exposes reusable templates, and automates approvals without code.

## Core value props
1. Prebuilt internal app templates for approvals, issue triage, and data entry.
2. Secure access control tuned for ops teams and non-technical admins.
3. Native connectors to databases, APIs, and spreadsheets.
4. Workflow automation with audit logs and embedded action buttons.
5. Faster rollout than custom Retool apps.
6. Predictable pricing with fewer per-seat fees.

## Pricing strategy
- Starter: for small ops teams who want internal tools with a single shared workspace.
- Growth: best for multi-team operators who need connectors, audit logs, and user roles.
- Scale: enterprise-grade access control, SSO, and premium onboarding.

## Why cheaper/better than Retool
- Simpler seat model for operations teams, not per-user engineering licenses.
- Templates built for internal processes instead of general-purpose UI assembly.
- Built-in governance and audit logging for compliance-minded companies.
- Focused onboarding for ops use cases reduces time to value.

## Messaging alignment for the landing
- Hero should promise internal tooling without engineering.
- Features should reflect templates, security, connectors, audits, automation, and fast deployment.
- Pricing should clearly position Starter, Growth, and Scale, with Growth highlighted.
- FAQ should answer security, connectors, pricing, and onboarding concerns.
